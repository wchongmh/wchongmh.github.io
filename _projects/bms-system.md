  </div>

      <li>Automated alarm management</li>
      <li>Hybrid wired/wireless data collection</li>
      <li>Energy consumption analytics</li>
      <li>Maintenance workflow integration</li>
      <li>Cloud-based analytics</li>
    </ul>
