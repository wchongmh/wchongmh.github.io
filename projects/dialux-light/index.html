This lighting design project for a mixed-use commercial building demonstrates the application of DIALux software to create compliant, energy-efficient lighting solutions for various spaces including offices, a supermarket, coffee shop, and circulation areas. The design meets all CIBSE SLL and UK regulatory requirements while incorporating modern control systems.

01 Introduction

As the lighting engineer for this project, I was tasked with evaluating and designing lighting systems for a two-story commercial building containing offices, a supermarket, and a coffee shop. The objective was to provide recommendations for lighting types and configurations based on CIBSE SLL standards while considering energy efficiency, maintenance requirements, and user comfort.

The design process utilized DIALux software for lighting calculations and visualizations, ensuring compliance with:

  • CIBSE SLL Lighting Handbook 2018
  • Health and Safety at Work Act 1974
  • Lighting at Work HSG38
  • Energy Efficient Lighting Good Practice Guide

03 Regulatory Requirements

The lighting design complies with all relevant UK legislation and guidance documents to ensure safety, energy efficiency, and optimal working conditions.

Energy Efficient Lighting Guide

Provides guidance on reducing energy consumption through proper lighting selection, controls, and efficient technologies like LED lighting.

Health and Safety at Work Act 1974

Requires employers to provide adequate lighting to ensure tasks can be performed safely by all employees.

Lighting at Work HSG38

Guidance on lighting requirements for different workplaces, including controlling glare, reflection, and shadow.

SLL Lighting Handbook

Authoritative reference for lighting design professionals, providing recommended lighting levels for various spaces and activities.

06 Project Reflection

This lighting design project provided valuable experience in applying theoretical knowledge to practical design challenges. Using DIALux software allowed for precise calculations and visualizations that ensured compliance with all relevant standards while optimizing energy efficiency.

Key Learnings

  • Importance of considering both quantitative (lux levels) and qualitative (user comfort) aspects in lighting design
  • Benefits of integrating natural light with artificial lighting systems
  • Value of intelligent control systems for energy efficiency
  • Need for careful luminaire selection based on space requirements
