This Hevacomp analysis examines a two-story office building in Glasgow, Scotland, with 11 rooms per floor totaling 22 spaces. The building features double-glazed windows, mechanical ventilation systems, and LTHW heating with CARADON STELRAD Elite K2 radiators. The design complies with UK Building Regulations and Scottish Technical Standards.

07 Conclusion & Recommendations

The Hevacomp analysis identified several opportunities to improve the building's energy efficiency while maintaining comfortable indoor conditions. Key findings include significant heat loss through infiltration (38%) and glazing (28%), with total building heat losses of 35,144W.

Recommended Improvements

Building Fabric

  • Improve wall insulation to achieve U-value of 0.23 W/m²K
  • Enhance window seals to reduce infiltration
  • Consider triple glazing for critical areas

Systems

  • Install thermostatic radiator valves (TRVs)
  • Expand supply/extract duct sizes as recommended
  • Implement smart controls for ventilation

Final Assessment

The designed systems meet all regulatory requirements including Building Regulations Part F and the Boiler Efficiency Directive. With the recommended improvements, the building could achieve 15-20% greater energy efficiency while maintaining excellent indoor environmental quality.
