Scottish Technical Standards Section 6

Building Performance Analysis IES VE Compliance Energy Modeling HVAC Optimization Renewable Integration Building Fabric Analysis

This project demonstrates the comprehensive energy compliance assessment for a two-story office building in Glasgow (ASHRAE Climate Zone 5A) with a total floor area of 1,182.07 m². The analysis was conducted using IES VE Compliance software to ensure compliance with Section 6 of the Scottish Technical Standards (2015-2023).

IES VE Compliance software interface

Design and analysis workflow

The building initially failed to meet Section 6 requirements with CO₂ emissions of 98.5 kgCO₂/m² (limit: 50 kgCO₂/m²) and energy consumption of 232.48 kWh/m². Through systematic improvements to building fabric, HVAC systems, lighting, and renewable integration, the final design achieved compliance with emissions of 12.2 kgCO₂/m² and energy consumption of 83.78 kWh/m².

01 Project Overview

The building consists of two primary floors (L00 with 22 rooms and L01 with 18 rooms) serving B1 office and workshop functions. The structure features reinforced concrete construction with heavy glazing and a southwest curtain wall. Initial analysis revealed significant non-compliance in multiple areas requiring comprehensive system redesign.

02 Regulatory Analysis

Section 6.1 of the Scottish Building Standards mandates specific energy performance criteria for office buildings, including maximum CO₂ emissions of 50 kgCO₂/m², proper thermal performance of building elements, and efficient HVAC and lighting systems. The initial design failed to meet these requirements in multiple areas.

Key Compliance Requirements

Critical Standards

  • Maximum annual CO₂ emissions: 50 kgCO₂/m²
  • Air permeability: 5 m³/hr/m² at 50 Pa
  • U-values meeting BS EN ISO 6946:2007 standards
  • HVAC systems meeting EN 15243 energy efficiency requirements
  • Lighting power density not exceeding 7 W/m²

03 Initial Analysis

The initial building assessment revealed significant non-compliance across all key metrics, requiring comprehensive system redesign to meet Section 6 requirements.

Key Deficiencies

  • CO₂ emissions: 98.5 kgCO₂/m² (limit: 50)
  • Energy consumption: 232.48 kWh/m²
  • Average U-value: 0.83 W/m²K (target: 0.27)
  • Air permeability: 10 m³/hr/m² (target: 5)

Inefficient Systems

  • Dual-duct VAV systems with poor efficiency
  • Induction systems in inappropriate areas
  • Local electric heaters with high energy use

Fabric Issues

  • External walls: 0.27 W/m²K (actual: 0.83)
  • Roof: 0.18 W/m²K (actual: 0.10)
  • Floors exceeding required U-values

04 Fabric Improvements

Comprehensive upgrades to the building envelope achieved significant energy savings and emission reductions through improved insulation and reduced air leakage.

Component Upgrades

  • External walls: Increased insulation to 95mm
  • Roof: Added polyurethane board insulation
  • Floors: Improved insulation and materials

Detailed Specifications

All building elements modified to meet or exceed required U-values with careful material selection and insulation upgrades.

Performance Impact

  • BER reduction: 22.8 kgCO₂/m²
  • U-value improvement: 0.83 → 0.27 W/m²K
  • Air permeability: 10 → 5 m³/hr/m²

05 HVAC System Optimization

Complete redesign of HVAC systems achieved substantial energy savings through strategic equipment selection and biomass integration.

Fan Coil Unit System

  • Replaced inefficient Dual-duct VAV and Induction systems
  • ECM motors for variable speed operation
  • Biomass-fueled LTHW boiler (η=0.7)
  • VRF cooling system (EER=2.55)

BER reduction: 17.7 kgCO₂/m² • TER reduction: 7.3 kgCO₂/m²

Radiator Circuit System

  • Installed in changing facilities and toilets
  • Biomass-fueled with 0.75 generator efficiency
  • Weather compensation control

BER reduction: 9.7 kgCO₂/m² • TER reduction: 0.4 kgCO₂/m²

Dual-duct VAV System

  • Retained for specific zones (cupboard, plant room)
  • Thermal wheel heat recovery (η=0.75)
  • Class D ductwork, Class L1 AHU

BER reduction: 15.5 kgCO₂/m² • TER reduction: 0.5 kgCO₂/m²

06 Lighting & DHW Optimization

Strategic upgrades to lighting and domestic hot water systems contributed significantly to overall energy reduction.

Lighting System

  • Power density reduced from 18.75 to 7 W/m²
  • Photoelectric dimming with back sensors
  • Stand-alone sensors (0.2 W/m² parasitic power)

BER reduction: 12.5 kgCO₂/m²

DHW System

  • Air-source heat pump (η=0.91)
  • Biogas-fueled with optimal controls
  • Designed for peak occupancy periods

BER reduction: 0.4 kgCO₂/m²

07 Renewable Energy Integration

The final compliance push was achieved through strategic integration of monocrystalline PV panels, providing the remaining emissions reduction needed.

Solar PV System

  • 4 monocrystalline panels (60m² each)
  • 30° south-facing orientation
  • Minimum yield: 850 kWh/kWp/year
  • Compliant with IEC 61215/61730
