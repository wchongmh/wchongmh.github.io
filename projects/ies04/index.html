Energy Compliance Analysis

Scottish Technical Standards Section 6

Building Performance Analysis IES VE Compliance Energy Modeling HVAC Optimization Renewable Integration Building Fabric Analysis

This project demonstrates the process of bringing a 1,182 m² Glasgow office building into compliance with Section 6 of the Scottish Technical Standards (2015-2023) through comprehensive energy modeling and system optimization using IES VE software. The analysis covers building fabric improvements, HVAC system upgrades, lighting optimization, and renewable energy integration.

01 Project Overview

The two-story office and workshop building in Glasgow (ASHRAE Climate Zone 5A) initially failed to meet Section 6 requirements with CO₂ emissions of 98.5 kg/m². Through systematic analysis and modifications, the project achieved compliance with emissions reduced to 12.2 kg/m², representing an 87.6% reduction while maintaining occupant comfort.

02 Compliance Requirements

Section 6 of the Scottish Building Standards (2015-2023) establishes stringent energy performance criteria for office buildings, including maximum CO₂ emissions of 50 kg/m² annually. The analysis focused on three key compliance areas:

Building Fabric

Mandatory U-values for walls (0.27 W/m²K), roofs (0.18 W/m²K), and floors (0.25 W/m²K) with air permeability ≤5 m³/hr/m² at 50 Pa.

HVAC Systems

Minimum seasonal efficiencies for heating (SCoP ≥2.5) and cooling (SSEER ≥2.5) with specific fan power ≤1.5 W/(l/s).

Lighting

Maximum lighting power density of 7 W/m² with appropriate controls and minimum luminous efficacy of 60 lm/W.

03 Initial Analysis

The initial energy simulation revealed significant non-compliance with Section 6 requirements:

  • CO₂ emissions: 98.5 kg/m² (vs 50 kg/m² limit)
  • Energy consumption: 232.48 kWh/m²
  • Average U-value: 0.83 W/m²K
  • Air permeability: 10 m³/hr/m²
  • Lighting power density: 18.75 W/m²

04 Building Fabric Improvements

Key modifications to building envelope components reduced heat loss by 62%:

  • External walls: Increased insulation from 77.8mm to 95mm (U-value: 0.27 → 0.21 W/m²K)
  • Internal partitions: Added 50mm insulation (U-value: 1.79 → 0.44 W/m²K)
  • Roof: Added 74.9mm polyurethane board (U-value: 0.10 → 0.18 W/m²K)
  • Air permeability: Reduced from 10 to 5 m³/hr/m²

Performance Impact

Building Emissions Rate (BER) reduced from 98.5 to 75.1 kgCO₂/m² (23.7% reduction) through fabric improvements alone.

05 HVAC System Optimization

The original HVAC systems were replaced with four optimized systems tailored to different zones:

1. Fan Coil Unit System

  • Applied to: Office areas, eating/drinking zones
  • Features: ECM motors, variable speed control
  • Efficiency: Seasonal COP 3.2
  • Impact: BER reduction of 17.7 kgCO₂/m²

2. Radiator Circuit System

  • Applied to: Changing facilities, toilets, circulation
  • Features: Biomass-fueled LTHW boiler
  • Efficiency: Seasonal efficiency 0.75
  • Impact: BER reduction of 9.7 kgCO₂/m²

3. Dual-Duct VAV System

  • Applied to: Cupboards, plant rooms
  • Features: Thermal wheel heat recovery
  • Efficiency: Seasonal EER 2.55
  • Impact: BER reduction of 15.5 kgCO₂/m²

4. Local Electric Heater Mech Vent

  • Applied to: Toilets, changing rooms
  • Features: Biomass heat source
  • Efficiency: SFP reduced from 2.6 to 1.1 W/(l/s)
  • Impact: BER reduction of 15.5 kgCO₂/m²

06 Lighting & DHW Optimization

Lighting System Improvements

  • Reduced lighting power density from 18.75 to 7 W/m²
  • Implemented photoelectric dimming with back sensors
  • Optimized luminous efficacy to 60-80 lm/W
  • BER reduction: 12.5 kgCO₂/m²

Domestic Hot Water System

  • Installed air-source heat pump with seasonal efficiency 0.91
  • Implemented optimum start/stop control
  • BER reduction: 0.4 kgCO₂/m²

07 Renewable Energy Integration

To achieve final compliance, the project incorporated 240 m² of monocrystalline PV panels:

  • 4 panels (60 m² each) at 30° south orientation
  • Annual yield: 850 kWh/kWp
  • Compliant with IEC 61215 and 61730 standards
  • Final BER reduction: 6.2 kgCO₂/m²

Final Building Emissions Rate: 12.2 kgCO₂/m² (87.6% reduction from initial)

Conclusion

The comprehensive energy optimization strategy successfully brought the building into full compliance with Section 6 of the Scottish Technical Standards. Key achievements include an 87.6% reduction in CO₂ emissions, 64% reduction in energy consumption, and improved occupant comfort through optimized HVAC and lighting systems. The project demonstrates the effectiveness of integrated building performance analysis in achieving stringent energy standards.
