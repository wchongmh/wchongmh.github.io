This project demonstrates the comprehensive energy analysis and modifications made to a Glasgow office building to achieve compliance with Section 6 of the Scottish Technical Standards. The process involved detailed simulation in IES VE software, building fabric improvements, HVAC system optimization, and renewable energy integration.

Design Working Process

The project focused on a two-story office and workshop building in Glasgow (ASHRAE Climate Zone 5A) with a total floor area of 1182.07 m². Initial analysis revealed significant non-compliance with Section 6 requirements, particularly in energy consumption (232.48 kWh/m²) and CO₂ emissions (98.5 kgCO₂/m²). Through systematic modifications, the building achieved full compliance with emissions reduced to 12.2 kgCO₂/m².

3 Compliance Requirements

Section 6 of the Scottish Technical Standards establishes rigorous energy performance criteria for office buildings, including maximum CO₂ emissions, thermal performance standards, and system efficiency requirements.

Section 6 Key Requirements

  • Annual CO₂ emissions ≤ 50 kgCO₂/m²
  • Energy efficiency assessed via National Calculation Methodology
  • Minimum thermal performance for building fabric
  • Efficient HVAC systems with low-carbon energy sources
  • Proper ventilation with minimal energy use
  • Efficient lighting meeting visual environment standards

Key Standards

Air Permeability

Required value of 5 m³/hr/m² at 50 Pa for non-domestic buildings (EN 15242)

Building Fabric

U-values calculated per BS EN ISO 6946:2007 with thermal bridge considerations

HVAC Systems

EN 15243 defines SCoP for heating and SSEER for cooling efficiency metrics

Lighting

Illuminance levels and power density requirements per NCM Activity Database

Building Fabric Improvements

Significant improvements were made to the building fabric to reduce heat loss and improve thermal performance:

  • External wall U-value improved from 0.2700 to 0.2093 W/m²K
  • Internal partition U-value reduced from 1.7888 to 0.4445 W/m²K
  • Exposed floor U-value improved from 0.2200 to 0.1730 W/m²K
  • Roof U-value optimized to 0.1800 W/m²K
  • Air permeability reduced from 10 to 5 m³/hr/m² at 50 Pa

These changes resulted in a 22.8 kgCO₂/m² reduction in Building Emissions Rate (BER).

HVAC System Optimization

The HVAC system redesign focused on zone-specific solutions incorporating biomass energy sources and heat recovery, achieving a 17.7 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER.

Replaced induction systems and dual-duct VAV in office and eating areas with fan coil units featuring ECM motors for better efficiency and control.

  • 17.7 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER
  • 7.3 kgCO₂/m² reduction in TER
  • Seasonal efficiency: 0.7
  • Equipped with heat recovery (Thermal wheel, 0.75 efficiency)

Implemented radiator circuits using water in changing facilities, toilets, and circulation areas for improved energy efficiency.

  • 9.7 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER
  • 0.4 kgCO₂/m² reduction in TER
  • Powered by LTHW boiler with biomass fuel
  • 0.75 generator efficiency

Installed dual-duct VAV system in cupboards and plant rooms for precise temperature control.

  • 15.5 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER
  • 0.5 kgCO₂/m² reduction in TER
  • Seasonal cooling EER: 2.55
  • Specific fan power: 1.5 W/(l/s)

Optimized local electric heater mechanical ventilation systems in toilet areas.

  • 15.5 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER
  • 0.5 kgCO₂/m² reduction in TER
  • Specific fan power adjusted from 2.60 to 1.10
  • Uses "Biomass: Meter 1" as heat source

Implemented efficient DHW system with air source heat pump (seasonal efficiency 0.91).

  • 0.4 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER
  • Optimized for peak occupancy periods
  • Biogas fuel source
  • Storage loss factor optimized

Lighting System Upgrade

The lighting system was comprehensively redesigned to improve efficiency:

  • Total wattage reduced from 18.75 W/m² to 7 W/m²
  • Office areas equipped with photoelectric dimming with back sensor
  • Other areas use manual switching
  • Parasitic power limited to 0.2 W/m²

These changes resulted in a 12.5 kgCO₂/m² reduction in BER.

05 Renewable Energy Integration

To achieve full compliance, the project incorporated monocrystalline silicon PV panels:

  • 4 PV panels totaling 240 m² (60 m² each)
  • South-facing orientation at 30° inclination
  • Minimum yield of 850 kWh/kWp per year
  • Compliant with IEC 61215 and IEC 61730 standards
  • Additional 11.91 kWh/m² energy production

The PV system provided the final 6.2 kgCO₂/m² reduction needed to achieve compliance.
