The analysis focuses on optimizing natural lighting while meeting BS EN 17037 standards, particularly important for Glasgow's predominantly overcast climate. The methodology combines traditional daylight factor calculations with advanced computer simulations to validate results.

01 Project Overview

The property at Market Road, Kirkintilloch features eight primary daylit spaces across two floors, with varying window orientations and external obstructions. Key challenges included north-facing rooms with adjacent buildings and optimizing south-facing spaces without overheating risks.

02 Design Methodology

The daylight analysis followed a dual approach: manual Daylight Factor calculations per BS EN 17037 and comprehensive Dialux simulations. This combination allowed for verification of results while accounting for Glasgow's specific climate conditions.

300 lux across 50% of area for 50% of daylight hours

Bedrooms ≥2% DF, Living areas ≥1.5% DF

03 Daylight Calculations

Manual Daylight Factor calculations followed the BRE methodology, considering room geometry, window dimensions, reflectance values, and external obstructions. Key parameters included:

Reflectance Values

  • Ceiling: 0.8
  • Walls: 0.65
  • Floor: 0.4
  • Window: 0.1

Transmittance

Clear double glazing: 0.87
Dirt factor: 0.9
Total T = 0.783

05 Recommendations

⚠️

Safety Improvements

  • Install anti-slip strips in low-light transition areas
  • Add motion-activated lighting in cloakroom and bathroom thresholds
🪟

Daylight Enhancement

  • Consider light shelves for south-facing windows
  • Use higher reflectance wall finishes in north-facing rooms
⚡

Lighting Controls

  • Implement photocell-controlled lighting in all daylit spaces
  • Use occupancy sensors in transitional spaces

06 Conclusion

The analysis confirms that most spaces meet minimum daylight requirements, with south-facing rooms performing best. Key findings include:

  • Bedroom 1 achieved highest DF (16.28% manual, 3.79% Dialux)
  • Cloakroom had lowest performance (0.76% manual, 0.72% Dialux)
  • Daylight-linked controls could save 225 kWh annually

Future designs should prioritize window placement in north-facing rooms and incorporate advanced lighting controls to maximize energy savings while maintaining visual comfort.
