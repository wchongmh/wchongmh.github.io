The AXA Center project features integrated plumbing and fire protection systems for a 30-story commercial office tower in Wan Chai. The design addresses Hong Kong's stringent building codes while optimizing for maintenance accessibility and system redundancy.

30-storey commercial office building at AXA Center

Map location of AXA Center, 151 Gloucester Road

This 30-story commercial office tower with 717.6m² typical floor plates presents unique challenges for vertical water distribution and fire protection. The design implements zoned systems with pressure management to maintain performance across all levels while complying with Hong Kong's Fire Services Ordinance and Water Supplies Department requirements.

1 Project Overview

The plumbing infrastructure serves 30 floors of office space with male, female, and disabled toilet facilities on each level. The fire protection system is designed to OH3 hazard classification with three sprinkler zones to accommodate the 123.85m building height.

06 Project Reflection

This project successfully integrated academic knowledge with practical design requirements for high-rise commercial buildings. The comprehensive systems design addressed all regulatory requirements while optimizing for maintainability and future flexibility.

Key Lessons

  • Practical application of BS EN 12845 and Hong Kong fire codes
  • Importance of pressure reduction valves in high-rise buildings
  • Benefits of zoned sprinkler systems for tall structures
  • Value of standardized layouts for future maintenance
  • Need for redundant pumping systems in critical applications
